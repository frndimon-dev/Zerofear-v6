Courses / Basic Scooty Training

Basic Scooty
Training.

15 Classes · Approx. 30 Min/Class · Complete Beginners · Non-Cyclists Welcome

Book Basic ScootyWhatsApp →

Who is this for?

Complete beginners and nervous learners.

যাঁরা জীবনে cycle চালাননি, first time two-wheeler handle করছেন, বা riding-এর সময় fear/hesitation অনুভব করেন—তাঁদের জন্য structured beginner-first progression.

What you will learn

01 · CLASS 1–3

Foundation & Vehicle Familiarisation

Vehicle familiarisation, sitting posture, handle control, accelerator understanding, front/rear brake basics and trainer-assisted movement.

HandleAcceleratorBrakeStart/StopPosture
Outcome: vehicle controls-এর initial hesitation কমানো এবং basic controls চিনে নেওয়া.
02 · CLASS 4–6

Balance & Basic Control

Basic balance, straight-line movement, slow-speed control, smooth acceleration, controlled braking and Start → Move → Stop repetition.

BalanceStraight RidingSlow SpeedBraking
Outcome: controlled movement and balance practice.
03 · CLASS 7–9

Turning & Directional Control

Left/right turns, handle movement, speed control during turns, indicator awareness and turning + braking practice.

TurningIndicatorSpeed ControlBraking
Outcome: controlled turning practice.
04 · CLASS 10–12

Practical Riding & Road Awareness

Road observation, basic road awareness, turning + braking, controlled movement, trainer-guided practical riding and weak-area correction.

ObservationRoad AwarenessTurningCorrection
Outcome: trainer-guided practical riding experience.
05 · CLASS 13–15

Confidence & Riding Preparation

Weak-area correction, repetition, braking/turning control, safety awareness, responsible riding habits and independent riding preparation.

CorrectionRepetitionControlConfidence
Outcome: a clearer understanding of personal progress and next-step practice.

Important

Learning time varies by learner. Completing a fixed number of classes should not be presented as a guarantee of independent riding ability; progression depends on the learner’s practice and readiness.

Courses / Advanced Scooty Training

Advanced Scooty
Training.

10 Classes · For learners with basic control · Practical riding & confidence progression

Book Advanced Scooty

Who is this for?

For learners who already have basic control.

যাঁরা scooter চালানোর basic control জানেন কিন্তু turning, traffic, road observation বা practical confidence আরও develop করতে চান।

10-class progression

01 · CLASS 1–2

Busy Road Introduction

Road observation, lane awareness, braking and controlled movement around busier environments.

02 · CLASS 3–5

Practical Road Situations

Turning, braking, speed control, observation and trainer-guided practical situations.

03 · CLASS 6–8

Advanced Practical Confidence

Weak-area correction, repeated practical exposure and more independent control practice.

04 · CLASS 9–10

Final Practical Preparation

Consolidate control, practical judgement and learner-specific weak areas.

Progression principle

Advanced does not mean “faster.” It means moving from basic control toward more demanding practical situations with appropriate trainer guidance.

Bike Training
Clutch & Gear.

15 Classes · Approx. 30 Min/Class · Basic cycle balance required

Book Bike Training
Requirement & disclaimer

Existing course material states that basic cycle balance is required. Completing this course is not a guarantee that a learner can safely operate every high-displacement or high-power motorcycle; vehicle weight and power differ.

15-class progression

01 · CLASS 1–3

Bike Familiarisation & Basic Control

Posture, handle, brakes, balance, start/stop and coordination.

BalanceHandleBrakeStart/Stop
02 · CLASS 4–6

Clutch & Gear Fundamentals

Clutch control/release, gear fundamentals, starting from stop, gear selection, low-speed control and clutch + brake coordination.

03 · CLASS 7–9

Clutch + Gear + Acceleration

Smooth acceleration, gear changes, braking, turning, start/stop and slow-speed coordination.

04 · CLASS 10–12

Practical Road Riding

Road observation, traffic awareness, turning, braking, speed control, positioning and practical situations.

05 · CLASS 13–15

Confidence & RTO-Oriented Preparation

Weak-area correction, confidence, repetition, clutch/gear coordination and RTO-oriented practical preparation where applicable.

Blog / Beginner Guide

সাইকেল জানেন না?
তবুও কি Scooty শেখা সম্ভব?

← Back to guides

সাইকেল না জানা নিয়ে ভয় পাওয়াটা common. The useful question is not “Can I magically balance?” but “Can I start with the right progression?”

Start with vehicle familiarity

Handle, posture, brake and accelerator সম্পর্কে familiarity আগে তৈরি করা যায়. তারপর start-stop and controlled movement.

Balance comes through controlled practice

Slow, repeated practice helps the learner understand how the vehicle responds. The training journey should not jump straight to traffic pressure.

When to move forward

Turning, braking and road observation gradually enter the progression. Learning time varies by learner.

Blog / Philosophy

Confidence Before Speed
Step-by-Step.

← Back to guides

Speed is easy to demonstrate. Control is harder to build. For a beginner, the useful sequence is understanding → control → repetition → practical experience → confidence.

Why this matters

When a learner is calm enough to brake, turn and observe, the next step becomes more meaningful. The goal is not to promise a fixed outcome; it is to create a clearer learning path.
